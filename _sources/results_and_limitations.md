# Results and Limitations

## Model Comparison (Current)
- CNN: strong baseline and smallest size
- MobileNetV2: best accuracy/F1 balance for deployment
- Hybrid: competitive but larger and slower in benchmark setup

## Limitations
- Domain shift expected from PlantVillage to real-field imagery.
- Classical segmentation can degrade under heavy clutter or poor lighting.

## Next Steps
- Field-image fine-tuning/domain adaptation
- Model optimization for edge/mobile deployment
- Expanded explainability and advisory guidance
